About

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Florida State University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside, in 2022, and a B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University in 2014.

My research is broadly in high-performance computing, with a focus on scientific data management, efficiency, and machine learning systems. I lead the development of SZ3, an R&D 100 Award-winning error-bounded lossy compression framework, and my work is supported by NSF and DOE/Argonne National Laboratory.

Positions are available in my lab. Please refer to the Hiring page for more information.

FSU is among the top 30 universities in the world in the field of HPC.

Research Interests

  • High Performance Computing
    Scalable algorithms and systems for supercomputers.
  • Scientific Data Management
    Reducing, storing, and analyzing data at extreme scale.
  • AI for Science
    Machine learning for scientific data and system design.
  • Systems for Machine Learning
    Efficient and scalable training and inference.
